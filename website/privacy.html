Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MusicCoach collects the following information to provide and improve our service:

  • Audio Data: We process audio from your device's microphone locally on your device to provide real-time pitch feedback. This audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.
  • Account Information: Email address, name (if provided), and subscription status.
  • Usage Data: Practice session duration, songs practiced, and accuracy statistics to show your progress.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide real-time pitch detection and feedback
  • Track your practice progress and improvement over time
  • Process subscription payments and manage your account
  • Send important updates about the app
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Provide customer support

3. Audio Privacy

Your audio is completely private. MusicCoach processes all audio data locally on your device. We never:

  • Record your practice sessions
  • Store audio files
  • Transmit audio to our servers
  • Share audio with third parties

The pitch detection algorithm runs entirely on your device in real-time.

4. Data Storage

Your practice statistics and progress data are stored securely:

  • Encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Stored on secure cloud servers (AWS/Google Cloud)
  • Access-controlled and regularly backed up
  • Retained as long as your account is active

5. Third-Party Services

MusicCoach integrates with trusted third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Manages subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Mixpanel: Analyzes app usage to improve features
  • Google AdMob: Displays ads in the free version

These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

6. Advertising

The free version of MusicCoach displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers for ad targeting
  • Ad interaction data
  • General location (city-level)

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no ads.

7. Children's Privacy

MusicCoach is intended for users of all ages. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
  • Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's information
  • Audio processing happens locally and is never stored

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Correct: Update inaccurate information
  • Export: Download your practice statistics
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@musiccoach.app

9. Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • Practice statistics: For the lifetime of your account
  • Account information: Until you delete your account
  • Backup copies: Up to 90 days after account deletion

You can delete your account anytime in the app settings.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure payment processing through Apple/Google

11. International Users

MusicCoach is available worldwide. Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We'll notify you of significant changes via:

  • In-app notification
  • Email to your registered address
  • Update on this website

Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Contact us:

  • Email: privacy@musiccoach.app
  • Support: support@musiccoach.app
  • Website: musiccoach.app

14. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For European Union users, we comply with GDPR:

  • Legal basis: Consent and contract performance
  • Data controller: MusicCoach, LLC
  • DPO contact: dpo@musiccoach.app
  • Right to lodge complaint: Contact your local data protection authority

15. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under CCPA:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

Summary: We respect your privacy. Your audio stays on your device, we collect minimal data to provide the service, and you have full control over your information.
