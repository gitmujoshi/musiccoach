Getting Started

How does MusicCoach work?
MusicCoach uses your device's microphone to listen to the notes you play in real-time. Our advanced pitch detection algorithm (based on the YIN algorithm) analyzes the frequency of your sound and compares it to the target notes in the piece you're practicing. You'll see instant visual feedback showing whether you're sharp, flat, or in tune.
What instruments work with MusicCoach?
MusicCoach works with any pitched instrument including violin, viola, cello, voice, flute, clarinet, saxophone, trumpet, and more. Any instrument that produces sustained pitched notes will work great! Percussion and rhythm instruments are not currently supported.
Do I need an internet connection?
No! Once you've downloaded songs or uploaded your audio files, you can practice completely offline. The pitch detection runs entirely on your device. Internet is only needed for downloading songs, syncing progress, or accessing premium features.

Using the App

How do I upload my own music?
In Premium: Tap the "+" button → "Upload File" → select an audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A) from your device. MusicCoach will analyze the audio and create a pitch reference for you to practice with. Make sure you have the rights to use any uploaded music.
What do the colors mean on the pitch display?
Green: You're in tune! Keep playing.
Blue line: The target pitch you should be playing.
Red dot: Your current pitch (when you're out of tune).
The closer your dot is to the line, the better your intonation!
Can I slow down the tempo?
Yes! Use the tempo slider at the bottom of the practice screen. You can slow down to 50% speed or speed up to 150%. The pitch detection will still work accurately at any tempo.
How do I loop a difficult section?
Tap the loop button, then drag the handles on the waveform to select the section you want to repeat. Hit play and MusicCoach will continuously loop that section until you turn off the loop.

Subscriptions & Billing

What's included in the free version?
Free users get: 10 minutes of practice per day, 3 demo songs, basic pitch detection, and tempo control. Ads are displayed in the free version.
What's included in Premium?
Premium includes: Unlimited practice time, upload unlimited audio files, advanced loop controls, detailed progress tracking, support for multiple instruments, no ads, and priority support.
How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → MusicCoach → Cancel Subscription
Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → MusicCoach → Cancel Subscription
You'll keep Premium access until the end of your current billing period.
Can I get a refund?
Refund policies are managed by Apple and Google. Contact App Store or Play Store support directly. Generally, refunds are available within 48 hours of purchase if you haven't used the app extensively.

Troubleshooting

The pitch detection isn't working correctly
Try these solutions:
1. Check microphone permissions: Settings → MusicCoach → Allow Microphone
2. Reduce background noise: Practice in a quiet room
3. Play closer to your device: Keep your phone 1-3 feet away
4. Play louder: Soft playing may be hard to detect
5. Check mic sensitivity: Try the sensitivity slider in Settings
6. Restart the app: Close completely and reopen
The app crashes or freezes
1. Update to the latest version from App Store/Play Store
2. Restart your device
3. Clear app cache (Settings → Apps → MusicCoach → Clear Cache on Android)
4. Ensure you have at least 500MB free storage
5. If issues persist, contact support with your device model and iOS/Android version
Audio playback is delayed or out of sync
1. Close other apps using audio
2. Disable Bluetooth audio devices (use device speakers or wired headphones)
3. Reduce buffer size in Settings → Audio → Buffer Size → Small
4. On Android, enable "Performance Mode" in Settings
My progress isn't syncing
Ensure you're signed in with the same account on all devices. Check your internet connection. Sync happens automatically when online, but you can force sync in Settings → Account → Sync Now.

Privacy & Security

Is my audio recorded?
No! MusicCoach never records, stores, or transmits your audio. All pitch detection happens locally on your device in real-time. Your practice sessions are completely private. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
What data do you collect?
We collect: email address (for account), practice session statistics (for progress tracking), and basic usage analytics (to improve the app). We never collect audio, personal information beyond email, or any identifying data from your practice sessions.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This will permanently delete all your data including practice statistics and uploaded files. This cannot be undone.
